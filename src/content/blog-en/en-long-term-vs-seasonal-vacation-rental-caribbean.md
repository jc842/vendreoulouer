# Long-Term vs Seasonal Vacation Rental: Comparing Yields & Operating Costs

Owners of Caribbean property must choose between steady residential leases and lucrative holiday rentals.

## Comparative Highlights

- **Seasonal Vacation Rental**: Generates peak daily rates from December to April with personal owner usage outside peak weeks, but requires professional concierge management (20-25% fee).
- **Unfurnished Long-Term Rental**: Highly stable cash flow with negligible vacancy, lower wear-and-tear, and minimal management overhead.
- **Regulatory Framework**: Vacation rentals require registration with local municipal authorities, whereas residential leases follow standard French civil code protections.

Balancing operational effort against return determines the optimal leasing route.
