# Determining Fair Market Value: Caribbean Valuation & Price-Per-Sq-Meter

Overpricing a property causes extended market exposure, while underpricing forfeits rightful equity.

## Valuation Pillars in the French Antilles

1. **Micro-Location & Sea Horizon**: An unobstructed ocean view commands a 20-35% premium over equivalent inland parcels.
2. **Structural Grade & Coastal Exposure**: Hurricane-rated concrete construction, cistern capacity, and resistance to marine salt air heavily influence valuation.
3. **Historical Notary Records (*DVF Database*)**: Relying on registered notary transaction prices rather than speculative portal asking prices.

Objective valuation accelerates sales and maximizes lender financing approvals.
